DoodleSprout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026
Developer: Salty creations
Contact: mindfulmastery786@gmail.com

Overview DoodleSprout is a children's drawing and early-learning application. This policy describes the app's current offline design. We do not operate a DoodleSprout account service, cloud gallery or analytics service.

Information stored on your device The app stores drawings, drawing strokes, progress, saved-picture titles and timestamps, and preferences such as language, voice guidance and handedness in its private storage on your device. This information is used to display and resume your artwork and remember your choices. Salty creations does not receive this information through the app.

Information we do not collect through the app The app does not ask for names, email addresses, account credentials, location, contacts, photos, voice recordings or advertising identifiers. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not send app activity or crash reports to Salty creations. It does not request internet, camera or microphone permission. We do not sell information collected through DoodleSprout or use it for advertising.

Children DoodleSprout is intended for children, with grown-up help for device settings and file management. The app does not provide chat, social posting, public profiles, advertising, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Please do not enter identifying information into artwork or include a child's personal information when contacting support.

Optional voice guidance Spoken guidance uses a matching, installed offline text-to-speech voice. The app selects voices that report that they do not require a network connection and does not use microphone input. If no matching offline voice is installed, drawing remains available without narration. Device voice software and any voice downloads are provided by your device's operating system or voice provider and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Exporting drawings If you choose to export a drawing, the app creates a PNG and opens the operating system's destination picker. The file goes to the location you select. That location may be on the device or with another storage provider. Exported files are outside the app's private storage; the selected provider's privacy rules apply. We do not receive a copy. Delete exported copies separately if you no longer want them.

Retention and deletion Local drawings and preferences remain until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Delete individual pictures from My art book. App-managed automatic backup is disabled. There is no DoodleSprout account or server record to delete. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not remove files you previously exported elsewhere.

Support email If a grown-up emails mindfulmastery786@gmail.com, we receive the sender's email address and the contents and attachments they choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer the request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Email is processed by our email provider under its own privacy practices. We retain correspondence while it is needed to handle the request and related follow-up. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us; information that must be retained for a legal obligation may be retained for that purpose. Do not send children's artwork containing personal information or other sensitive information.

Store and device services Amazon, the device operating system, voice providers and storage destinations may independently process information under their own policies, including information about store purchases, downloads, device diagnostics or files you choose to export. This policy covers DoodleSprout and Salty creations, not those independent services.

Changes and contact We will update this policy if the app's data practices change and revise the effective date. Questions about DoodleSprout privacy can be sent by a grown-up to Salty creations at mindfulmastery786@gmail.com.
